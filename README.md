### CHEM-000124: Entanglement-assisted selection of bimolecular products
- **Subfield:** Molecular reaction dynamics and spectroscopy
- **Open:** Experimentally control product-state branching by deliberately entangling reactants, and distinguish the advantage from ordinary reactant-state preparation.
- **Status:** A 2025 calculation predicted complete control of product rotational parity for an ultracold KRb + KRb example. Experimental implementation and generalization remain open.
